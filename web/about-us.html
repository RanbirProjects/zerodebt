About Zerodebt Solution

We exist to help lenders recover what is owed and to help borrowers move towards a debt-free tomorrow, fairly and lawfully.

Our story

Recovery doesn't have to be a fight

Too often, old loans end in stalemate: lenders write off value and borrowers carry stress for years. Zerodebt Solution was founded on a simple belief: most accounts can be resolved when the right analysis meets a respectful conversation.

Our name says what we aim for, and our tagline says how we get there: Analyse, Resolve, Recover.

  • Lender-first reporting, borrower-first conduct.
  • Every account has a documented trail from first contact to closure.

Our mission

To maximise lawful recovery for lenders while giving every borrower a clear, dignified path to closing their loan.

Our vision

A financial ecosystem where aged debt is resolved early, openly and fairly, so credit keeps flowing to those who need it.

Our promise

No threats, no harassment, no shaming. Just honest analysis, clear options and consistent follow-through.

Our values

What guides every account we handle

Integrity

How a case moves

From first look to closure

STEP 01

Portfolio intake & data review

We receive your accounts securely, validate the data and flag gaps in contact and documentation.

STEP 02

Segmentation & strategy

Accounts are grouped by ageing, ticket size and traceability, and each group gets a tailored approach.

STEP 03

Borrower engagement

Trained, courteous agents explain dues, listen to constraints and propose workable repayment options.

STEP 04

Resolution & collection

Agreements are documented and payments flow directly to the lender through traceable channels.

STEP 05

Reporting & closure

You get reconciled reports and borrowers get closure confirmation, so the loop is truly closed.

Compliance commitment. Our agents are briefed on fair-practice norms: reasonable calling hours, clear identification, no abusive language and a visible grievance path for every borrower.
