Our Expertise

Specialised recovery and resolution capability across every major loan category.

Retail loan recovery

Personal, vehicle, two-wheeler, consumer-durable and education loans make up the bulk of aged retail books. Small tickets need an efficient, high-touch yet respectful approach.

  • Bucket-wise strategy (30 / 60 / 90 / 180+ days past due)
  • Multi-channel outreach: calls, SMS, email and visits
  • Promise-to-pay tracking and follow-up discipline

Best for

Banks, NBFCs and fintech lenders with large volumes of small-ticket delinquent accounts.

Credit card dues

Card receivables age quickly and borrowers often juggle several cards. We focus on clear statements, affordable plans and settlement where appropriate.

  • Statement reconciliation and dispute handling
  • Instalment conversion and structured settlements
  • Closure letters shared after final payment

Best for

Card issuers and banks with unsecured card receivables in later delinquency buckets.

MSME & business loans

Business defaults usually have a story: cash-flow gaps, delayed receivables, market shifts. Understanding it lets us propose plans the business can actually keep.

  • Business-viability and cash-flow review
  • Customised restructuring proposals
  • Senior-level engagement for larger tickets

Best for

Lenders to small and medium businesses, traders and self-employed borrowers.

Skip tracing & field verification

When a borrower can't be reached, the account stalls. Our tracing blends data validation, digital tracing and verified on-ground visits.

  • Contact and address validation
  • Geo-tagged, time-stamped field reports
  • Updated borrower records returned to the lender
Ask about tracing

Best for

Portfolios with high "not reachable" rates, old contact data or relocated borrowers.

Settlement & restructuring

For many aged accounts, a fair settlement or revised schedule beats years of stalemate. We negotiate within the lender's approved parameters and document everything.

  • Policy-aligned one-time settlement offers
  • Revised tenure / instalment plans
  • Written terms and payment via traceable channels only

Best for

Lenders with approved settlement policies and borrowers wanting a clear route to closure.
