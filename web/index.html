Our approach

Analyse. Resolve. Recover.

A simple, transparent three-step method that turns stuck accounts into closed ones.

1. Analyse

We study the portfolio: ageing, ticket size, contact quality, borrower profile and legal position, then prioritise by recoverability.

2. Resolve

We engage the borrower with empathy and offer realistic options such as part-payments, restructuring or settlement, documented in writing.

3. Recover

Payments are collected through traceable channels, accounts are reconciled and the lender receives clear, auditable reports.
